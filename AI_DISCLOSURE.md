# Generative AI use disclosure

Our team used generative AI throughout this project as a learning and development aid. We used it to help the group brainstorm possibilitie and find datasets, help organise the project and working environment, explain Deep Learning concepts and the behaviour of functions, libraries and error messages, and assist with code. We also used it to suggest clearer and more concise wording for team notes and project documentation.

At times, generative AI also offered suggestions about the modelling approach, evaluation and possible interpretations. We treated these as material for discussion and verification. We never accepted any ouput at face-value (except for coding blocks). The team took full responsability for defining the problem, choosing and justifying the dataset and model, checking the results of code blocks, interpreting the evidence, and deciding the conclusions and next steps. 

The submitted AI Canvas and final interpretation reflect the team's own work and judgment.
