# Team Project Brief

Team size: 4 or 5 students per team. You may choose your own team.
Deadline: September 20, 2026, by 23:59.
Class 5: The full session will be dedicated to working on the team project.
Project objective:
Each team should identify a relevant real-world problem and develop a Deep Learning model to address it. The project should clearly connect the business or operational context, the available data, the modelling approach, and the decision the model is intended to support.

### Teams should:

- Define the problem and explain why it is relevant.
- Select an appropriate dataset and target variable.
- Build and test a Deep Learning model.
- Evaluate the model using appropriate metrics.
- Interpret the main results and limitations.
- Explain why Deep Learning is an appropriate choice for the problem, rather than - simply using it because it is part of the course.
- Discuss how the model could support a real business or operational decision.

Just to support you in preparing the final assignment outputs, you will need to:

(i) demonstrate that you have identified a relevant problem;

(ii) choose a dataset that can support the development of a model and that is clearly connected to your problem; and

(iii) explain and interpret the final results of your model.

For (i), you will need to complete the AI Canvas available in Class 5: https://cocreatelearninglabs.com/pbs/ai/canvas.html

For (ii) and (iii), you will need to submit a notebook containing your model, the link to the dataset, and your final comments and interpretation of the results.
